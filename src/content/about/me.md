I’m a design leader with over 15 years of experience in UX, product design, and visual storytelling.

This site brings together my UX case studies, visual and motion work, photography, and sound practice so you can see how the different parts of my creative work inform each other.

Over the years I’ve:

- Led and rebuilt design teams and departments
- Introduced DesignOps and Product Design practices inside a software consultancy
- Shipped products across macOS, iOS, iPadOS, Android, and the web
- Worked closely with engineering, product, and leadership to deliver measurable outcomes

Outside traditional product work, I stay creatively active through:

- **Visual & Motion** – graphic design, motion graphics, and video  
- **Photography** – observational work that sharpens my sense of composition and timing  
- **Sound & Music** – ambient sound design, field recording, and experimental composition  

All of those threads feed directly into how I think about interaction, pacing, and narrative in product experiences.
