I’m a multidisciplinary designer and creative practitioner with over 15 years of experience in UX, product design, and visual storytelling.

My work sits at the intersection of product, systems, and creative exploration. This site brings together UX case studies alongside visual, motion, photographic, and sound-based work—showing how these practices continuously inform one another rather than existing in isolation.

## Product & UX Practice

Professionally, I’ve spent years working inside software and product teams, helping organizations ship thoughtful, durable products.

Over the years, I’ve:

- Led, rebuilt, and mentored design teams and departments  
- Introduced DesignOps and product design practices within a software consultancy  
- Shipped products across macOS, iOS, iPadOS, Android, and the web  
- Partnered closely with engineering, product, and leadership to deliver measurable outcomes  

## Creative Practice

Alongside product work, I maintain an active creative practice outside traditional software constraints.

- **Visual, Motion & Video** — graphic systems, motion graphics, and short-form video  
- **Photography** — observational work focused on composition, rhythm, and light  
- **Sound & Music** — ambient sound design, field recording, and experimental composition  

## How It Connects

These disciplines directly shape how I approach UX and product design—informing my sensitivity to pacing, transitions, narrative, and the emotional texture of interaction. For me, good products aren’t just usable systems, but carefully composed experiences.

*Different tools. One way of thinking.*
