## Situation

During my nearly two years at Michigan Software Labs, I had the opportunity to transform the design department by introducing a user-centered design philosophy and implementing cross-functional team workshops to enhance product design and team collaboration. While I can't share specific projects due to confidentiality agreements, I can highlight one public project, Hearsee Mobility, which focused on creating an accessible navigation solution for visually impaired users, discussed in a dedicated case study. Beyond that, I want to share the broader story of how I helped revolutionize the design process at Michigan Software Labs and the valuable insights I gained as Design Manager.

## Company Overview

Michigan Software Labs is a software consultancy that partners with a diverse range of clients, including Fortune 500 companies, universities, and non-profits. During my tenure, I led design efforts on software solutions across various platforms, including IoT, iOS, Android, iPadOS, and Web. One particularly rewarding challenge was developing an IoT solution for a manufacturing client, where we successfully improved system reliability and user experience by simplifying complex workflows for factory operators.

The projects we worked on spanned industries such as:

- Media streaming and content delivery  
- Multimedia platform development  
- Indoor positioning and navigation systems  
- Educational technology for higher education  
- Online learning platforms and digital courseware  
- Industrial automation and manufacturing  
- Supply chain management and logistics  
- Accessible technology and inclusive design  

## Transforming the Design Department

A significant part of my role focused on modernizing the design department, which initially faced challenges such as inconsistent processes, siloed efforts, and a lack of user-centered focus. To address these issues, I transformed our approach through initiatives like:

### Introducing Product Design

I implemented a product design philosophy that shifted our work to be more user-centered and holistic, ensuring we addressed the full scope of user needs while gaining greater client buy-in and involvement. This required a cultural shift toward an integrated and user-focused approach.

### Establishing Design Ops

I introduced a design operations framework to create consistent processes, improve collaboration, and scale design effectively. This initiative — which did not exist before my arrival — led to a **25% reduction in project delivery time** and dramatically improved cross-functional alignment.

### Incorporating Design Thinking

Working with leadership, I embedded design thinking into our software development process. In one instance, using design thinking in an indoor navigation project improved user journey clarity and significantly raised user testing scores.

### Team Building and Restructuring

I rebuilt the team with an emphasis on diversity, cohesion, and filling skill gaps so we could better meet the varied needs of clients across industries.

## Key Accomplishments

### Team Charter Development  
Defined goals, values, and expectations to unify the design team and set clarity for the rest of the organization.

### Design Ops Implementation  
Created consistent workflows, improved alignment, and reduced delivery time by 25%.

### Career Path Development  
Developed a four-track growth framework for designers at all stages of their careers.

### User-Centered Product Design  
Infl uenced stronger client relationships by focusing deeply on user needs and business goals.

### Creative Team Techniques  
Implemented improvisation-based workshops to strengthen collaboration and ideation.

## Reflection

Our work at Michigan Software Labs spanned diverse industries — from higher education and e-learning to manufacturing and accessibility — each demanding a unique approach. I'm especially proud of how we shifted from fragmented design efforts to a cohesive, structured practice grounded in user needs and thoughtful process.

Implementing product design principles and building a design operations foundation fundamentally changed how the team worked. It increased efficiency, improved user feedback, and raised product quality across the board.

This experience reinforced my belief in the value of:

- design thinking  
- structured yet flexible processes  
- and a diverse, collaborative design team  

Although many projects remain confidential, the measurable improvements and cultural transformation demonstrate the impact of strategic design leadership.
