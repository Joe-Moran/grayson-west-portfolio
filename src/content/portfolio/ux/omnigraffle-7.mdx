## Situation

OmniGraffle is a vector-based diagramming and illustration tool for macOS and iOS. As Creative Director for the end-to-end redesign of OmniGraffle 7, I led research, design operations, and a multidisciplinary team of UX designers, UI designers, and researchers.

This work resulted in a **23% increase in download-to-trial conversion** and positioned OmniGraffle 7 as a **top-10 grossing app** on the App Store for seven weeks.

## Task

  <LightboxImage

The goal of the version 7 redesign was to improve efficiency and usability for creative professionals by streamlining workflows, reducing complexity, and making core features easier to access.

Key improvements introduced in OmniGraffle 7 included:

- **Artboards** for managing complex projects  
- **Bezier point editing enhancements**  
- **Convert lines and text to shapes**  
- **Expanded keyboard shortcuts**  
- **A redesigned onboarding experience**

## Research and Analysis

For the OmniGraffle 7 release, I led the UX team through a combination of qualitative and quantitative research.

### Research Methods

- **User interviews** with experienced OmniGraffle users  
- **Usability testing** to observe real workflows and friction points  

#### User Interviews (Pre-Development)

- **Participants**: 15 OmniGraffle 6 users  
- **Key findings**:
  - 60% requested improved Bezier editing
  - 50% cited the lack of artboards as a deal-breaker
  - 70% emphasized speed and efficiency

#### Usability Testing

- **Participants**: 10 users  
- **Task success rate**: 65%  
- **Average time on task**: 4.2 minutes  
- 55% of users felt workflows took too long

These insights clearly indicated the need for both **new features** and **workflow simplification**.

## Collaboration with Engineering

We worked closely with engineering to validate feasibility and align design intent with technical constraints.

One major challenge was implementing **artboards** within an existing drawing model that wasn’t designed for them. By treating artboards similarly to layers while preserving performance, we delivered a flexible solution without compromising stability.

## Results

- **Download-to-trial conversion** increased by:
  - 23% on the Mac App Store
  - 16% on the Omni website
- **Sales** increased by 33% in the first three months
- **Workflow efficiency** improved by 12%
- OmniGraffle 7 remained a **top-10 grossing app** for seven weeks

## Reflection

This redesign reinforced the importance of deeply understanding both **technical constraints** and **user workflows**. It also highlighted the value of clear communication between design and engineering — especially when introducing foundational changes like artboards.

The result was a more powerful, flexible, and professional-grade tool that better served OmniGraffle’s core audience.
