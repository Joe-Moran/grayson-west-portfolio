## Caves and Terraces — Motion Graphic
Caves and Terraces is a custom motion graphic created for Lake Tahoe in Depth (2025), a 20-minute immersive film produced for the UC Davis Tahoe Environmental Research Center and shown at the UC Davis Tahoe Science Center. I led the visual concept, design, and animation, translating geologic research into a clear, spatial narrative. The piece visualizes long-term fault movement within the Tahoe Basin, showing how caves and terraces on the west side sit higher than same-age features on the east, confirming the basin’s east-side-down fault motion. This 2025 version updates the original 2011 film using contemporary motion and visual pacing.

## The Omni Group Video Progam
### My Role
Founded Omni’s in-house video department to support users after purchase and clearly communicate real-world use cases across the product line. I initially handled everything end-to-end, including writing, directing, shooting, screen-casting, editing, and producing. Once the program was established, I hired and led a three-person team, providing creative, art, production, and marketing direction as the department scaled. 

### 24 Brix
Customer story film featuring Michael Werk, created to show how OmniFocus supports the day-to-day operation of Camp 1 Vineyard in Sonoma, California. I led the creative direction and narrative approach, and handled all on-site production, including cinematography and editing, shaping the story from concept through final cut.
