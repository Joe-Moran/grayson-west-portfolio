## Art

The Omni Group attended many Macworlds before it's demise. These are some examples of Omni's design for the 2003 and 2008 events. What is not being communicated here is the size of these designs. The OmniGraffle and OmniWeb designs were 8 feet tall and the generic Omni Group booth was 20 feet tall.
