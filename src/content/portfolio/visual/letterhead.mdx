## The Omni Group Letterhead & Business Card System
This is a complete letterhead and business card system designed for The Omni Group. The goal was clarity and consistency across everyday communication, using a restrained color palette, strong typographic hierarchy, and a flexible layout that scaled from formal correspondence to personal contact details. It balanced professionalism with the quiet confidence of a product-focused software company.
