## Overview

### Througout the years, I created a variety of t-shirt designs for The Omni Group. Some of these were for fun and some for marketing, and some for events. The goal was to translate software personality into physical artifacts—using iconography, restraint, and humor.

## OmniGraffle Swiss Army Knife
<figure>
  <LightboxImage
  src={omniGraffleShirt}
  alt="OmniGraffle Swiss Army Knife t-shirt"
  caption="OmniGraffle, just like a swiss army knife."

One of The Omni Group's most popular software applications. OmniGraffle is vector digramming and drawing tool, and the mulitfunction of of the Swiss Army Knife was meant to represent it vast feature set.

## OmniGraffle Giraffe Shirt

This was very much an inside joke within the hall of Omni. OmniGraffle and OmniGiraffe somehow become the synomous with the application. I tried to make a mythical animal for the company to wear and enjoy.

## OmniFocus Subway Map Shirt

  <LightboxImage
  src={omniFocusShirt}
