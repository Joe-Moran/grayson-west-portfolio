## The Story
Organic Forms & Sonic Shapes Vol. 1 is the debut release from Particle Bunni, a two-person ambient-electronic collaboration between multidisciplinary artists Terrance Stearns and Grayson West.

Blending electronic sound generation, guitar, analog instruments, voice, and field recordings, the project explores the intersection of mysticism, psychoacoustics, and digital folklore. The work sits between composition and texture—focused less on genre and more on atmosphere, process, and slow-unfolding forms.

This album marks Particle Bunni’s first release and establishes the project’s foundation: exploratory, tactile, and intentionally open-ended.
